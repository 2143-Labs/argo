Repo at `~/repos/edge-tofu`, branch `main`, commit `6ea25cd`. Local only: no remote, no credentials, no provider calls.


**Acceptance — verified**
1. `render --manifest fixtures/home.json --slot s1 --provider hcloud` emits YAML that parses (`yq`) and carries every declared port, including `25`, `45000-45127`, `9987`, `34197`. `machine.network.interfaces[0].wireguard.listenPort` is 51820.
2. The conflict fixture fails with `port tcp/25565 (b) overlaps tcp/25565-25565 (a)`.
3. `tofu fmt -check -recursive` passes; `tofu validate` succeeds in `providers/hcloud`, `providers/digitalocean` and `modules/edge`.
4. Nothing outside the repo was touched; no state, no apply.

Two design corrections found while building:
- **Hostname ownership is per host *and port*.** `m.2143.me` is legitimately HTTP on 80/443 and Stalwart on 25/587/993; a hostname-only check was wrong.
- **The edge firewall selects by port, not protocol**, so the rendered list is de-duplicated (`3478` appears once) and the TCP/UDP split is kept in a comment.

**Explicitly out of scope:** image builds and signing, OpenBao integration, and any `tofu apply`. The rendered config is still a skeleton: `talosctl validate` is the Phase 3 gate, and `machine.type` is unresolved until Phase 0 answers it.

## 6. Consequences of the separation (D24)

- **The repo stays in `2143-Labs`, so no second runner.** The org-level `edge-deploy` scale set (D6) can serve `2143-Labs/edge-tofu`; isolation comes from the dedicated scale set and its namespace-scoped RBAC, not from a separate organisation. The custom-job runner used for other CI must not be eligible for `edges`.
- **Signing identity is** `https://github.com/2143-Labs/edge-tofu/...`; the cosign verification policy pins that path, and the 2143-Labs App registration already covers the org.
- **DigitalOcean moves to a new business account.** That gives a fresh transfer pool (no Tor relay), a separate API token and a business invoice; the personal account keeps DOKS, `status-1` and the relay untouched. Accounts and teams are free — the only cost is the Droplet itself. New accounts need a payment method and may start with lower resource limits until verified; confirm region/size availability before Phase 3.
- **Hetzner is a new business account** under `labs@2143.me`: separate billing and token, nothing shared. Confirm ASH1 stock and the current CPX11 price on that account before locking the module.
- **DNS is unaffected:** the controller and its deSEC token stay at home, so the edge accounts still cannot repoint a name.
- **Billing splits along the business/personal line**, which is what an LLC wants: edge spend on the business, home and DOKS on the personal account.
